# Selahpa Runasimi t'ikrayninman yanapakuy

Ñawiriqkuna, Hebreo yachaqkuna, t'ikraqkuna, Runasimipi wiñaqkuna —
allin hamusqa kachkankichik. Qankunam kay simita máquinamanta aswan
yachankichik. Kay qillqaqa imaynatas yanapawankiku chaytam willan.

## Pantayta willay, yuyayta quy

- Waqaychanapi huk **Issue** kichay:
  <https://github.com/selah-project/selah-qu>
- Icha, kikiyki allichayta atispaqa, huk **Pull request** kichay.
- Icha qillqamuwayku: <support@selahproject.com>

## Ima kanan chay willakuypi

- Maypichus kasqan: liwru, t'aqa, siq'i (kayhina, `genesis 1:1`).
- Kunan imachus qillqasqa kasqan, imaraykutaq pantasqa kasqan.
- Imachus qillqasqa kanan, yachaptiykiqa.

## Willañiqi allichanapaq kamachikuykuna

- Huk willañiqi = huk siq'i: `<liwru>/<t'aqa>/<siq'i>.json`.
- **Simi t'aqakunaq yupaynin sut'ichaykunaq yupayninwan kaqllam
  kanan**: sapa Hebreo simi t'aqaman huk sut'ichayllam qukun; ama
  pisiyachiychu, ama yapaychu.
- **⟨את⟩ sanampakunaqa wiñaypaqmi** — ama chinkachiychu, ama
  yapaychu.
- Sutikunaqa D1 nisqan hinam sayanku (qhaway
  `docs/methodology/translation-discipline/qu.md` hatun
  waqaychanapi): **Yahwe**, **Elohim**, **El**, **Eloah**,
  **Adonay**, **Shaday**, **Tsevaot**, **Yah**. Yupaychana rantin
  rimayqa manam Sutiq tiyananpi sayanchu — manam *Señor*, manam
  *Dios*, manam *Taytacha*, manam *Apu*, manam *Jehová* Sutiq
  tiyananpi nitaq Elohimpa tiyananpipas.
- **Sutiq ñawpaqninman manam yupaychana rimay k'askanchu**: manam
  *Tayta Yahwe*, manam *Apu Yahwe*, manam *Señor Yahwe*, manam
  *Taytacha Yahwe*. Ichaqa qhipaman k'askaq simikunaqa rimaypa
  kamachiyninmi, manam chinkachiychu: *Yahwepa*, *Yahweman*,
  *Yahwewan*, *Yahwemanta*, *Yahwemi* — llapan allinmi. Sutiq sapinqa
  mana hukchasqam qhipan.
- שאול **Sheol**mi, manam *Ukhu Pacha*chu.
- **-mi**qa kay t'ikraypa allpanmi; **-si**qa Hebreo kikin huk
  rimaqpa nisqanta willaptillanmi, manam Yahweq rimasqanpichu;
  **-chá**qa Hebreo mana yuyaychakuptin mana yaykunchu.
- Yapasqa simikunaqa ⟨⟩ ukhullapim kanku, Runasimi rimay
  mañakuptillan.
- Manam mayqin Inglés icha Español simipas siq'ipi qhipanchu — *the*,
  *and*, *God*, *LORD*, *el*, *la*, *de*, *que*, *Dios*, *Señor* icha
  huk kaqkunapas — ⟨⟩ ukhupipas manam.

## Tupuna

Qillqaman cheqaq kayqa misk'i ñawiriymanta aswan hatunmi. Huk
allichay rimayta misk'ichaspa Hebreo qillqamanta karunchawaptinchikqa,
manam yaykunchu. Aswan cheqaqta ruwaptinqa, mañakuykiku: apamuy.

---

**English.** Contributions welcome: open an Issue or Pull request at
<https://github.com/selah-project/selah-qu>, or write to
<support@selahproject.com>. One file = one verse; unit count equals
gloss count; ⟨את⟩ markers are never deleted or added; the D1 Names
(Yahwe, Elohim…) never yield to a title — not *Señor*, not *Dios*, not
*Taytacha*, not *Apu*, not *Jehová* at the Name or Elohim seat. No
title is welded in front of the Name (never *Tayta Yahwe*, *Apu
Yahwe*, *Señor Yahwe*), but case and focus suffixes on the Name are
grammar, not erasure — *Yahwepa*, *Yahweman*, *Yahwewan*,
*Yahwemanta*, *Yahwemi* are all correct and the stem stays intact.
שאול is **Sheol**, never *Ukhu Pacha*. The evidential ground is **-mi**;
**-si** only where the Hebrew itself reports another's speech, never at
Yahwe's own speaking. Supplied words only in ⟨⟩; no English or Spanish
words survive in the line, brackets included. Letter-faithfulness
outranks readability.
